## 2 写作修辞

**Abstract 逐句功能骨架**（:103，"两步走"版）：
1. Context："Shadow removal is an essential task for scene understanding."
2. Problem（失效模式命名 + 前引 Figure 1）："Many studies consider only matching the image contents, which often causes two types of ghosts: color in-consistencies in shadow regions or artifacts on shadow boundaries~(as shown in Figure 1)."——给失败模式起专名 "ghosts"，标题由它而来。
3. 总纲："In this paper, we tackle these issues in two ways."
4. First：DHAN（growth dilated conv、无下采样、双层次聚合）。
5. Second 的论证句（数据瓶颈量化）："we argue that training on a limited dataset restricts the textural understanding of the network...Currently, the largest dataset contains 2k+ shadow/shadow-free image pairs. However, it has only 0.1k+ unique scenes since many samples share exactly the same background"——用 2k+/0.1k+ 数字对比立论。
6. 方法二：SMGAN。
7. 结果："outperforms other state-of-the-art methods by a large margin."
8. 代码 URL 收尾（AAAI 惯例的开源声明）。

**Introduction 段落功能序列**（:107-132）：
P1 自然现象开场（非应用开场，与 1907 不同）："Shadows are a common phenomenon in nature. Cast shadows, which often appear in the opposite direction of a light source..."（:108），落到下游任务动机（object detection/tracking）；P2 先行工作按时间线四连句（\citeauthor...\shortcite 句式，:109）；P3 缺口段以 "However, current deep learning-based techniques have two major drawbacks. First,... **贡献清单范式**：3 条：框架（named DHAN，"with the help of shadow detection"）→ 数据合成（"design and train a Shadow Matting GAN...Finally, we train shadow-related tasks using augmented datasets"）→ 实验（"outperforms other state-of-the-art methods, especially on the visual quality"）。组件式三件套（结构 + 数据 + 实验），每条带目的从句；无 "Conceptual Discovery" 式高层包装。

## 3 选题品味

**创新类型**：Tier 2 为主（为"影子去除"这个特定底层任务定制结构：growth dilated conv 全程无下采样保边界——"there is no down-sampling or stride convolution in the context encoding, the low-level details can be well preserved"，:169）+ Tier 6 数据/基准驱动的一面（SMGAN 造数据扩域）。两个 Tier 2 缺口都论证到根因：结构缺口归因于"前人网络不是为去影设计的"（:118），数据缺口归因于"2k+ 对只有 0.1k+ 场景"（:118）。

**问题从哪来**：失效模式命名学——"two types of ghosts: color in-consistencies in shadow regions or artifacts on shadow boundaries"（:103），每个 ghost 各归因一个根因（色彩不一致 ← 数据场景太少；边界伪影 ← 结构下采样）。矛盾内核沿用 1907：共享语义 vs 区域差异（:120 斜体句）。

**实验与消融哲学**：
1. **递进式结构消融**：CAN → +LA → +DLA 三行递进（:369-371），每加一层组件给因果解释（:343 "our feature layer aggregation~(+ LA) technique greatly improves the quality of the shadow regions. This is because the hierarchical mixed features will force the network to better understand multiple contexts"）。
2. **与替代机制正面对比 + 可视化取证**：和 gated convolution 比 attention map（fig8，:343-344 "when the shadow region is large enough, the local gated convolution only gains the knowledge from its sibling convolution, causing the failure to capture the whole image context"）。
3. **中间产物单独评测**：合成影子质量用 LPIPS/SSIM/PSNR 立表自证（:380）。
4. **跨域 zero-shot 泛化实验**：ISTD 训练的模型直接测 SRD/SBU，"without retraining on their specific training datasets"，RMSE 9.82 vs 11.9（:400-401）——证明合成数据扩展了"场景理解"而非过拟合。
5. **下游任务外溢**：合成数据反哺 shadow detection，在 SBU 上与专用检测器比 BER（:315）。
6. 实现细节含速度：0.2s/image on TITAN V（:292）；指标扩展：在惯用 RMSE 外加 PSNR-S/SSIM-S "to evaluate whether it can produce high-quality shadow removal results"（:292）。
7. 公平性声明显式写出："all the results and figures are provided by the authors or taken from the original papers"（:304）。

**与他其他论文的关系线索**：这是 1907.06406 观察句 + attention loss 配方的**任务平移**：斜体观察句 "share the same semantic information and...only needs to learn the shadow"（:120）= 1907 "share the same semantic information...the differences can only be found in the spliced region"（main.tex:452-453）的换词版；attention 监督从 L₂（1907 method.tex:167-170）换成 BCE（:215-220），并在 1907 的实验里其实试过两者（experiment.tex:122）——配方沉淀复用。数据侧从"手工合成数据集"升级为"GAN 合成数据管线"，且 SMGAN 学的是 matting（I_shadow = I_matting × I_free，:272），为后续生成式/蒙版路线铺路。代码同在 vinthony 名下。

## 4 Idea 设计

**Idea 原型**：主 = 矛盾驱动（两个失效模式各指认一个被忽视的根因）；次 = **逆向任务迁移**——把"去影"反过来做"加影"："if we train the network to synthesize shadow images from the corresponding masks and shadow-free images, the network will also produce the novel shadows according to the novel mask. It is much easier to collect the required data"（:123）。双向任务互喂（合成的数据训练去除）是他把"造数据"制度化的关键一步。

**问题表述句式**：仍是**观察陈述式**，无设问句。模板："X 与 Y 共享同一 [语义信息]，[任务] 只需学习 [差异部分]"：
- :120 "*the shadow and shadow-free images share the same semantic information and shadow removal specifically and only needs to learn the shadow*"
- :272 引 DeShadowNet 观察："I_shadow = I_matting × I_free"——把观察固化成一行物理公式再在此之上做合成。
辅以"数据经济学"论证句式："It is much easier to collect A than B"（:123）、"it is far easier to collect the natural shadow-free images and random masks than to collect the corresponding shadow/shadow-free/shadow-mask sets"（:238）。

**本文局限可能催生的后续**：(a) 明示假设边界："we assume the scenes contain only cast shadows with the objects outside the scene"（:124）——自阴影/复杂软阴影留给后续；(b) 合成影分布与真实仍有差距的讨论段（:283 Discussion 位置）+ zero-shot 泛化实验（:400）→ 自然催生"更大规模合成/真实域差距"问题，与其后续走向生成模型（style transfer、video harmonization 等）一致；(c) attention loss 监督仍要 GT mask → 无 mask 场景仍开放。
