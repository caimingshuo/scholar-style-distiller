## 2 写作修辞

**Abstract 逐句功能骨架**（main.tex:450-457，无量化数字版六句闭环）：
1. Context："Image composition is one of the most important applications in image processing."
2. Problem："However, the inharmonious appearance between the spliced region and background degrade the quality of the image."（失效模式具体到视觉缺陷）
3. 任务定义句（签名式）："Thus, we address the problem of Image Harmonization: Given a spliced image and the mask of the spliced region, we try to harmonize the ``style'' of the pasted region with the background."
4. Gap："Previous approaches have been focusing on learning directly by the neural network."
5. 观察转折（签名式）："In this work, we start from an empirical observation: the differences can only be found in the spliced region...while they share the same semantic information and the appearance in the non-spliced region."
6. 方法："Thus, in order to learn the feature map in the masked region and the others individually, we propose a novel attention module named Spatial-Separated Attention Module (S²AM)."
7. 扩展宣言："we make a big step for harmonizing the composite image without the specific mask under previous observation."
8. 结果两句：模块级 SOTA + 方法级 SOTA 各一句（"performs better than other state-of-the-art attention modules"; "via criteria from multiple points of view"）。无数字、无代码 URL（code 挪到作者脚注）。

**Introduction 段落功能序列**（intro.tex，实际 5 段 + 贡献）：
P1 应用切入：Photoshop/智能手机让普通人也能拼图（intro.tex:34，从生活场景而非学界趋势开场）；P2 问题机理 + 方法谱系：人眼靠 color/texture/luminance 差异识破 → 手工特征（Lalonde、Sunkavalli）→ 深度方法（Zhu、Tsai）各给一句局限（intro.tex:35-40）；P3 核心观察段，以斜体宣言句 "Different from the previous methods, our key observation is: *For image harmonization task, the model needs to learn the dissimilarity...and ensure the consistency...*" 开头，并用图像取证文献（相机内部特征、JPEG 压缩、噪声）给观察背书（intro.tex:83-84）；P4 框架走读，Firstly/Then/Moreover/Finally 四步（intro.tex:86-90）；P5 贡献清单（见下）。

**贡献清单范式**：`\item[*]` 星号圆点 4 条（intro.tex:93-101）：模块 → 两个变体框架 → 推广到无 mask → 实验总结。每条都是"提出/设计 X + 机制从句"（"to learn the features in the foreground and background area by hard-coded masks individuality"），属组件式贡献写法，但第 2 条把设计意图（"fits the intention of the difference in the spliced area"）写进条目，不是纯流水账。

**Related Work 组织法**（related.tex）：分类法，3 个 `\textbf{}` run-in 标题段落（Image Harmonization / Attention Mechanisms / Image-to-Image Transformation），每段按时间线过一遍前人，**段尾必以 "However, ..." 划界**：related.tex:8 "However, these methods are designed for image in-painting task specifically."；related.tex:9 "However, these attention mechanisms are focus on learning the representation on the high level computer vision task other than digital image processing with low-level features change."。对前人温度中性、指局限时给技术性理由（GP-GAN "focuses more on blending the edge of the two different images rather than the style transform"，related.tex:5），无人身化贬词。

**标志性短语**（≥2 例）：
- "make a big step"：main.tex:455 "we make a big step for harmonizing the composite image without the specific mask"；intro.tex:90 "Finally, we make a big step for image harmonization without giving mask as well."
- "As far as we know, (we are) the first..."：method.tex:47 "As far as we know, we are the first to insert the attentive structure for the module with the hard-coded mask."；intro.tex:90 "As far as we know, our method is the first approach for harmonizing the composite image without mask by deep learning."
- "It is clear that / It is obvious that" 高频收束实验段：experiment.tex:39 "It is clear that our method outperforms other methods to a large extent."；experiment.tex:41 "It is obvious that our approach gains better results when the bed or person is the spliced region."
- "Notice that" 补充限定：method.tex:47、131、150 三处。
- 早期英文瑕疵（自写痕迹）："out-perfumes"（experiment.tex:65）、"It is obviously that"（intro.tex:64 注释与 1911 同款）、"causing better results"（method.tex:13）。

**标题与方法命名**：标题为动宾 + by 模块式，13 词，无冒号副标题（main.tex:374）。命名家族化：S²AM（带上标的缩写）派生出 S²ASC / S²AD 两个插入位置变体且各自命名（method.tex:127-129 "We call this type of connection *Spatial-Separated Attentive Skip-Connection* (S²ASC)... ## 3 选题品味

**创新类型**：Tier 2 极简机制替代——用 hard-coded mask 先验 + 三个 SE 式通道门控（G_bg/G_mix/G_fg）替代"端到端直接学习"，核心公式一行（method.tex:40-43 `y = M×[L(G_fg(x)) + G_mix(x)] + (1-M)×G_bg(x)`）；同时携带 Tier 1 式观察句（语义共享、差异只在区域内）作为立意支点。

**问题从哪来**：从失效模式的视觉缺陷出发——"the inharmonious appearance between the spliced region and background"（main.tex:450）；方法论根源是卷积核全局共享与区域编辑任务的矛盾："the traditional convolution filter...share the kernel parameters over the whole image, which benefits higher-level tasks to look for a given feature everywhere...However, for the image-to-image transformation in the certain region...it shows a relatively poorer performance"（method.tex:37）。

**实验与消融哲学**：
1. **plug-in 通用性对照**：模块插入 DIH/pix2pix/Unet 三个 backbone 全部涨点（experiment.tex:39 "RASC module get better numerical results when it is plugged into three baseline structures"）。
2. **参数量对齐的公平性控制**："we increase the channels in each levels of the original Unet to match the parameters of our Unet+S²AM model for a fair comparison"（experiment.tex:185），外加 CONV-Block 对照。
3. **原子化消融**：-G_bg/G_mix/G_fg、-G_mix、-Gaussian Mask、+6 layers、-Learning block 五个独立开关汇成一表（experiment.tex:226-251，tableevaluation.tex:19-23）。
4. **机制可视化验证假设**：三层 attention 通道响应热图，"there are more white blocks in G_fg than G_mix and G_bg in the S²ASC₁ module while the opposite phenomenon is shown in S²ASC₃. This fact perfectly explains our assumption"（experiment.tex:262）——用可视化闭环回观察句。
5. **超参扫描**：attention loss 的 L₂ vs BCE、比例 0.01×/0.1×/1×/10× 画 PSNR-epoch 曲线定 1:10（experiment.tex:122，psnr.pdf 五条曲线）。
6. 无 GT 场景补 AMT user study（experiment.tex:65）。
7. **自建数据集并承诺公开**：S-COCO 43k + S-Adobe5K 36k，"We will public these two synthesized datasets for further research"（method.tex:185-204）。
8. 推理速度入正文：0.012s/image on 1080 GPU（experiment.tex:7）——底层视觉的效率意识。

**与他其他论文的关系线索**：核心观察句（共享高层语义、只学区域差异）+ mask 先验 attention + attention loss 配方，在 1911.08718 中几乎原样迁移到阴影去除；本文是无 mask 版块（spatial attention + attention loss）为后续"预测 mask/蒙版"路线埋种子；conclusion.tex:5 明示模块外溢方向："can also be adapted to other computer vision tasks easily with regional differences, such as *Free-Form Image Inpainting* and *Semantic Image Synthesis*. We believe it is a promising direction for our future work."

## 4 Idea 设计

**Idea 原型**：主 = 矛盾驱动（全局共享卷积 vs 区域化外观编辑）；次 = 简化驱动（用 hard-coded mask 这个"免费先验"替代学习式 attention——"they provide a strong prior knowledge to the neural network"，method.tex:51）。非迁移/尺度/基准驱动。

**问题表述句式**：**观察陈述式**而非设问式，模板为 "X 与 Y 在共享 [语义/非区域外观]，差异只在 [区域]，因此只需分开学习 [区域差异] / 保持 [一致性]"：
- intro.tex:83 "*For image harmonization task, the model needs to learn the dissimilarity in the appearance of the spliced region and ensure the consistency in the non-spliced regions and high-level features*"
- main.tex:452-453 "we start from an empirical observation: the differences can only be found in the spliced region between the spliced image and the harmonized result while they share the same semantic information"
收敛链：应用痛点（拼图不调和）→ 机理（图像取证视角：差异在低层特征）→ 一句话设计原则（分而治之）。

**本文局限可能催生的后续**：(a) 无 mask 版依赖 attention loss 监督且 mask 预测不总准（method.tex:150 "the predicted mask is not always accurate"）→ 催生对 mask/matting 显式建模的后续（1911 的 SMGAN 正是学 shadow matting）；(b) conclusion 自荐模块可迁移到 inpainting/语义合成（conclusion.tex:5）→ 任务横移模板；(c) 数据全靠合成（style transfer 伪造）→ 造数据升级为造数据管线。
