## 2 写作修辞

**Abstract 骨架**（00_abstract.tex:3）——实证/benchmark 式 5 步，同样零量化大数字：
1. 背景："The vision and language generative models have been overgrown in recent years."；
2. 矛盾："However, these methods often use a few metrics, \eg, FVD or IS, to evaluate... We argue that it is hard to judge the large conditional generative models from the simple metrics since these models are often trained on very large datasets with multi-aspect abilities."；
3. 方案："Thus, we propose a novel framework and pipeline for exhaustively evaluating the performance of the generated videos."；
4. 展开：700 prompts → 17 指标四方面（"in terms of visual qualities, content qualities, motion qualities, and text-video alignment"）→ 人偏好对齐回归系数；
5. 验证："our final score shows a higher correlation than simply averaging the metrics, showing the effectiveness of the proposed evaluation method."

**Introduction 段落功能序列**（01_intro.tex）：
- P1 大模型繁荣（:4-5 ChatGPT/SD/SDXL）；
- P2 T2V 进展 + 瓶颈句："However, all current large text-to-video (T2V) models only use previous GAN-based metrics like FVD for evaluation, which only concerns the distribution matching..., other than the pairs between the text prompt and the generated video."（:9）；
- P3 洞见句："Differently, we argue that a good evaluation method should consider the metrics in different aspects, \eg, the motion quality and the temporal consistency."（:10），并补充闭源模型"只能拿到输出视频"的评测困境（:10）；
- P4 方法走读："We make the very first step to evaluate the general T2V models. In detail, we first build a comprehensive prompt list... Second, we assess... from four aspects..."（:18-20，First/Second 步进）；
- 贡献清单 3 条 itemize（:24-28）：①"make the first step" + prompt 基准；②四方面评估 + 人对齐 + 相关性验证；③"we also discuss several conclusions and findings, which might also contribute to further innovation"——把 Findings 本身当第三贡献。

**Related Work 组织法**（02_related.tex）：2 小节（T2V Generation and Evaluation / Evaluations on Large Generative Models）；T2V 小节按"VAE/GAN→diffusion→商业服务→开源模型"时间线列举（:14），对对手零贬义、纯中性列举；划界句 "However, a fair and detailed benchmark for evaluating these methods is still lacking."（:16）；T2I 评测小节末尾收束："However, these methods still work for T2I evaluation or language model evaluation. For T2V evaluation, we further consider the quality of motion and temporal consistency."（:26）。

**标志性短语**（≥2 例）：
- "We make the very first step to..."：01_intro.tex:18 "We make the very first step to evaluate the general T2V models"；01_intro.tex:25 "We make the first step of evaluating the large T2V model"；10_conclusion.tex:5 "we take the first step towards evaluating large, high-quality T2V models"——同一短语全文三现，是明确的自我定位口头禅。
- "We argue that..."：00_abstract.tex:3；01_intro.tex:10；04_metrics.tex:9 "we argue they are unsuitable for general T2V generation cases"——洞见句固定用 argue 引出。
- "we believe..."：04_metrics.tex:93 "we believe the motion quality is a major difference from other domains, such as image"。

**贡献清单范式**：3 条，第 3 条是"发现清单"型贡献（01_intro.tex:28），对应正文 Finding #1-#6。

**标题与方法命名**：`EvalCrafter: Benchmarking and Evaluating Large Video Generation Models`（_constants.tex:7）——"-Crafter" 词缀 + 冒号卖点副题，与 TaleCrafter 成系列；子组件命名朴素功能化（SD-Score、OVQ/TVA/MQ/TC 四方面缩写，figs/correlation.tex:5）。

## 3 选题品味

- **创新类型**：Tier 3 深度实证 + 基准驱动——不提新生成模型，而是 700 prompts 基准 + 17 指标 + 人偏好对齐回归 + leaderboard（00_abstract.tex:3；03_method.tex:10 "we collect 700 prompts as our initial version for evaluation with careful annotation"）。
- **问题从哪来**：矛盾驱动——模型能力多维化 vs 评测仍停在单一分布指标 FVD/IS（00_abstract.tex:3；01_intro.tex:9）；再加一个现实约束：闭源服务（Gen2/Pika）只能拿到输出视频（01_intro.tex:10 "some models are not publicly available and we can only get access to the generated videos, which further increases the difficulties in evaluation"）。
- **实验与消融哲学**：以"与人的一致性"为最终裁判——Spearman ρ / Kendall φ 相关性是核心验证手段（tables/correlation.tex:49 "Correlations between some objective metrics and human judgment"），对齐系数在 unseen 20% 样本上验证（05_results.tex:99 "shows a better correlation on the unseen 20\% samples than directly averaging"）；并且**把基准本身当实验对象做消融**：附录专设 "Adequacy of 700 Prompts"、"Benchmark Stability and Noise Variables"（tables/stability.tex:18 "Results' changes after noised"）、"Dependence on Pre-trained Models"、"Costs of Evaluation"（12_appendix.tex:109-131 章节树）——对基准做鲁棒性/依赖性扫描，这是 benchmark 论文的"消融等价物"。
- **与他文关系线索**：评测对象直接包含同门 VideoCrafter（tables/compare_metrics.tex:63 列名 "VideoCrafter"，VideoCrafter1 即 Cun 组作品）；GPT-4 第二次充当系统组件（TaleCrafter 的 S2P ↔ 本文 prompt 生成与自检，03_method.tex:25）；"camera motion 无法用文本控制"的 Finding #4（05_results.tex:133-134）直接给可控生成（他自己的主线）出题。

## 4 Idea 设计

- **Idea 原型**：主 = 基准驱动（定义 T2V 评测新战场，让后来者上排行榜）；次 = 矛盾驱动（单指标 vs 多维能力）+ 现象驱动（6 条 Findings 是论文的实质产出，如 "Finding \#3: Users prioritize visual appeal over T2V alignment"（05_results.tex:126-128）、"Finding \#6: Larger motion amplitude doesn't ensure user preference"（:138-140）——两条都反直觉）。
- **问题表述句式**：设问式小节标题——"What Kind of Prompts Should We Generate?"（03_method.tex:42 章节树）；回答方式是数据先行：先爬 600k 真实用户 prompt 过滤到 200k，做词长/词频/词类分析，再定四元类 human/animal/object/landscape（03_method.tex:14-16）——"用真实用户数据回答设计问题"的收敛链。
- **局限催生的后续**：Limitation 节 (i)(ii)(iii) 编号自曝（05_results.tex:143-149）：①700 prompts 太少（"the real-world situation is very complicated"）→ 扩规模/开源众包 prompt；②运动质量难评（"in the era of multi-model LLM..., we believe better and larger video understanding models will be released and we can use them as our metrics"）→ MLLM-as-metric 评测的明确预告；③标注者少（"may introduce some bias"）→ 扩标注池。Finding #4（camera motion 全军覆没）则是给自己可控生成主线出的题。
