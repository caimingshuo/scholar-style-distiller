## 2 写作修辞

- **Abstract 逐句骨架**（corpus report 摘录 + `sec/intro.tex` 同源）：(1) 任务定义句 "Video omnimatte seeks to separate a foreground object, along with its associated effects… into a single coherent layer"；(2) 对手痛点句 "Existing methods typically rely on slow, multi-stage, or inference-time optimization pipelines that fail to fully exploit powerful generative priors"；(3) **Key insight 句（本文招牌）** "Our key insight is that, if a video inpainting model can be finetuned to remove the foreground-associated effects, then \textit{it must be inherently capable of perceiving these effects}, and hence can also be finetuned for the complementary task"；(4) 意外失败句 "However, although naïvely finetuning… can produce high-quality alpha mattes, it fails to capture associated effects"；(5) 分析归因句 "Our systematic analysis reveals this arises because effect-related cues are primarily encoded in specific DiT blocks…"；(6) 方法句（Dual experts 两长句）+ 消融句 + SOTA 句 "Experiments demonstrate that \name sets a new state-of-the-art… significantly outperforming baselines in both quality and efficiency"。共 9 句左右，超出六句模板，多出的两句全给了"失败→分析"这条因果链——**把'发现问题-分析问题'写进 Abstract 是本篇最独特的骨架**。
- **Introduction 段落功能序列**（`sec/intro.tex`）：P1 任务价值与人工抠像痛点（L4-8）→ P2 Omnimatte 任务沿革 + 一句总括对手缺陷（L10-15，"However, these follow-ups typically rely on slow, multi-stage, or inference-time optimization pipelines that fail to fully exploit powerful generative priors"）→ P3 提出框架 + key insight（L34-42）→ P4 **意外失败现象**（L44 "our initial experiments with this straightforward setup revealed a critical and unexpected challenge"）→ P5 机制分析（L46-50 "Intrigued by this phenomenon, we conduct a systematic analysis…"）→ P6 解决方案 Dual Expert + 效率（L52-57）→ P7 实验宣言（L65，"Experiments confirm that this classic \emph{coarse-to-fine} strategy…"）→ 3 条贡献 bullets（L68-76）。**"现象驱动"的 intro 写法：先承认朴素方案失败，再用分析翻盘**。
- **Related Work 组织法：加粗小标题分派别 + 每派末尾点缺陷 + 终段统一收束到本文**。`sec/related.tex` 三个 `\noindent\textbf{...}` 派系："Foreground Isolation and Matting"（末尾 "they tend to fail to capture foreground-associated effects"）、"Video Matting with Associated Effects"（末尾 "a significant drawback is the reliance on restrictive motion assumptions"）、"Video Inpainting for Object Removal"（末尾 "these methods cannot eliminate the associated effects… often leaving residual artifacts"）；随后一段 "While recent methods can estimate the background…, using this pseudo-background for foreground separation still remains challenging. On one hand… On the other hand…"，最后一句 "In this work, we propose the first unified framework that…" 收束。对前人温度中性偏尊重（"pioneering work~\cite{lu2021omnimatte}"、"rapid progress of visual generative models"），批评只落在范式性质（慢/两阶段/假设强）不落具体论文。
- **标志性短语**（≥2 例）：
  1. "Our key insight is that…, then it must inherently possess the ability to…"（`sec/intro.tex:38-40`，同句复现于 Abstract 与 `sec/conclusion.tex` "Our key idea is to repurpose a powerful video inpainting model that has been proofed to be capable of perceiving the associated effects"）——**互补任务/能力反推式洞察句**。
  2. "the first unified, end-to-end"（`sec/intro.tex:34` "we present the \emph{first unified}, \emph{end-to-end} framework"；Abstract "the first unified, end-to-end video omnimatte method"；teaser caption "\emph{the first, end-to-end video omnimatte} method"）——**"first + 范式形容词"三连击**。
  3. "Intrigued by this phenomenon, we conduct a systematic analysis"（`sec/intro.tex:46`）+ "Our systematic analysis reveals…"（Abstract）——**把 systematic analysis 挂嘴边**。
  4. "Taming"（标题动词；`sec/method.tex` 小节名 "Repurpose Inpainting Diffusion for Omnimatte"；Conclusion "adapting large-scale generative models to complex, detailed video decomposition tasks"）——**驯服/改造大模型语系**。
- **贡献清单范式：3 条 bullets，第 1 条抢"first"范式声明，第 2 条给分析发现，第 3 条给方法机制**（`sec/intro.tex:70-75`："We introduce the \emph{first end-to-end video omnimatte} method…"；"We perform a structural analysis revealing that the standard inpainting pretraining objective inherently conflicts with the preservation of foreground effects, strongly motivating our key designs"——把消融/分析写成与实验同级的贡献）。

## 3 选题品味

- **创新类型：Tier 2（极简机制替代复杂工程）+ 一层 Tier 1 式的机制认知**。核心动作是把 Gen-Omnimatte 的"背景生成→测试时优化"两阶段流水线压成一个端到端前馈模型（`sec/related.tex` 末段 "we propose the first unified framework that simultaneously predicts the background layer and separates the foreground…, all within a single end-to-end model"），并附赠 30 倍效率（300s→10s，Fig.1 图内标注；正文 "reducing the inference time from several minutes to under 10 seconds"，PDF 第 6 页）。
- **问题来源：从自己/他人论文的矛盾中长出来**。痛点是 "effects" 在 matting 与 inpainting 两个社区之间没人管（`sec/related.tex` 两派各自的失败句）；关键原料是 Gen-Omnimatte/ObjectDrop 已证明"inpainting 模型能擦除效果"——存晓东把它反转为"能擦除⇒必能感知⇒可改为保留"。这是典型的**对互补任务的能力重估**，而非新数据/新 benchmark 驱动。
- **实验与消融哲学：分析先于设计，消融验证假设而非堆指标**。方法章里专设 3.2 "Block-Wise Analysis of Effects Association"（`sec/method.tex:77`），定义 Effects Association Score（公式 3-4，`sec/method.tex:87-125`），用曲线谷底把 DiT 分三段，声称 "perception-then-elimination" 假设（`figs/analysis_figure.tex:6`）——**先测出"效果感知住在中层块"，再决定 LoRA 只加在哪**。消融四件套：attention mask 三配置（`sec/experiments.tex:54-58`，明确写 "We attribute this issue to the masking strategy itself, rather than to the partial application of LoRA"——排除混淆变量）、Branch 位置（"Our finding is consistent with the conclusion from our layer-wise analysis"）、Dual Expert 两专家单独 vs 合用（(d)/(e) 变体）、阈值 τ 扫描画 MSE trade-off 曲线（`figs/threshold_ablate.tex:4` "users can decrease it for higher matte precision or increase it for more prominent associated effects"——把超参讲成用户可调旋钮）。补训数据消融也做成假设检验："Fine-tuning a general video model (WAN Fun) instead of an inpainting model leads to performance degradation… This ablation confirms that an inpainting foundation is critical"（`supp/figs/ablate.tex:4`）。
- **数据哲学：小而合成的训练数据 + 大模型先验**。"Our training data is synthetically generated by compositing foreground videos with high-quality alpha matte annotation over background videos"（`sec/experiments.tex:10-14`，8,000 iters、2×H100 的轻量训练）；supp 图 caption 直接升华 "it successfully decomposes challenging in-the-wild videos… including reflections and smoke. This highlights the model's ability to bridge a substantial domain gap by learning a true separation principle"（`supp/figs/train_data.tex:4`）。**用先验换数据是贯穿他体系的底层信念**。
- **与其他论文的关系**：(a) 直接站在 Gen-Omnimatte（lee2025generative）肩膀上反用其模型，teaser/正文全程以它为唯一 optimization-based 对手；(b) LoRA 微调 DiT 的手法与其 VideoCrafter2（chen2024videocrafter2，见其 related work 引用列表 `sec/related.tex`）以来的"微调而非重训"一脉相承；(c) Conclusion/局限明示外推路线："we will explore the application of our block-wise analysis and adaptation framework to more advanced and larger-scale generative models"（`sec/conclusion.tex` 末段）——下一篇的钩子已埋好。注意：存晓东本人早期工作即 matting（如 RobustVideoMatting 语料 2108.xxxxx），本篇是"回到 matting，但用生成式先验重做"的回旋镖式选题。

## 4 Idea 设计

- **Idea 原型：矛盾驱动（主）+ 现象驱动（辅）**。矛盾内核一句话："能擦除效果的模型必然感知了效果，所以同一模型可以微调成保留效果的互补任务"（`sec/intro.tex:38-40`）；现象层是朴素 LoRA 全块微调丢效果的意外失败（`sec/intro.tex:44`），由此触发 block-wise 探针实验。两层叠加：先有互补性矛盾（idea 骨架），后有"哪个块管效果"的观测（idea 血肉）。
- **问题表述句式**："if a video inpainting model can be finetuned to remove X, then it must be inherently capable of perceiving X, and hence can also be finetuned for the complementary task"——**「如果能做 A（更易的互补任务），则必具备做 ¬A 的能力」的能力反推条件句**，与 VideoCrafter2 的"能生成好图像⇒空间模块已有好先验"同构（见 2401 卡）。
- **收敛链**：视频分层分解太难（人工抠像贵）→ Omnimatte 效果没人端到端做 → 借 inpainting 模型的互补能力 → 朴素微调失败（效果丢失）→ 分析定位效果敏感块 → Dual Expert 分噪级接管。**失败不是绕开的弯路，而是方法章的正中心**。
- **本文局限催生的后续**：(1) 局限自认 "it is intrinsically linked to the capabilities of the base inpainting model"（`sec/conclusion.tex`），且 failure case 归因到基座（`supp/figs/failure.tex:4` "it naturally fails when the underlying inpainting model itself fails"）→ 换更强基座/更大 DiT 复用 block-wise 分析；(2) 训练只有合成伪阴影 → 真实多效果（反射、烟雾、飞溅）数据构造；(3) τ 是人工旋钮 → 自动 per-video 噪声调度；(4) "Fantastic Omnimatte and Where to Find Them" 这类弃用标题暗示他还在想"哪里能找到效果"的通用探测问题。
- **可复用的设计动作**：把 SOTA 流水线的两个阶段缝进同一个 DiT 的采样调度里（前段用 A 专家、后段用 B 专家），几乎零额外成本拿到两种能力——「用采样时间轴做专家路由」是其标志性工程-科学混合手法。
