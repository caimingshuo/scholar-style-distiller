## 2 写作修辞

### Abstract 逐句骨架（corpus report §3 全文）
1. 任务定义："Color constancy aims to restore the constant colors of a scene under different illuminants."
2. 矛盾一（传感器域差）："However, due to the existence of camera spectral sensitivity, the network trained on a certain sensor, cannot work well on others."
3. 矛盾二（数据少）："Also, since the training datasets are collected in certain environments, the diversity of illuminants is limited for complex real world prediction."
4. 总起句（工程双轨式，非常规"insight"句）："In this paper, we tackle these problems via two aspects."
5. 方法一："First, we propose cross-sensor self-supervised training... train the network by randomly sampling the artificial illuminants in a sensor-independent manner for scene relighting and supervision."
6. 方法二："Second, we analyze a previous cascaded framework and present a more compact and accurate model by sharing the backbone parameters with learning attention specifically."
7. 收束=量化卖点（无展望/升华句）："Experiments show that our cross-sensor model and single-sensor model outperform other state-of-the-art methods by a large margin... with only 16\% parameters of the previous best model."

### Introduction 段落功能序列（subfiles/introduction.tex）
- P1（:20）宏观背景：从人眼恒常性 → 计算 CC → AWB 实用价值（"accurate white balance is also necessary for high-level tasks, such as image classification and semantic segmentation"）。
- P2（:36-47）物理建模铺垫：先给积分式 Eq.1（含 CSS 函数），再化简到 von Kries 模型 Eq.2，然后摆出三重痛点：标注贵（需放色卡）、数据少（"there are only 0.2k--0.3k images per sensor"）、跨传感器域差（"most previous methods can only work well for a specific sensor"）。
- P3（:52）方法总览："To overcome aforementioned problems, as shown in Figure~\ref{fig:xcc}, we first propose a novel training technique, called \textit{self-supervised cross-sensor training}..."。
- P4（:53）附加模块：lightweight head，"reducing 50\% of the parameters while keeping accuracy"。
- P5（:55-66）3 条贡献 bullets：每条=机制+立刻给结果（"Once trained using randomly generated labels, our model achieves state-of-the-art performance on cross-sensor evaluation."；"using only 16\% of the parameters of the existing best model"）。

### Related Work 组织法（subfiles/relatedwork.tex）
- 分类法开场："Existing methods on color constancy can be approximately categorized into two classes: traditional statistics-based and deep learning-based methods."（:3）。
- Deep CC 内用 \textbf{段落小标题}分派：Illumination Regression and Classification / White Patch (Point) Localization / Pre-Training / Cross Sensor CC / Compact Networks。
- 每段以 "Differently, our ..." 收束划界（3 处）："Differently, our pre-training aims to regress the illuminant directly, which is simple and effective."；"Differently, our method leverages the knowledge from larger-scale datasets by randomly sampling illuminants."；"Differently, the proposed model is fully convolutional and obtains the state-of-the-art performance while using relatively fewer parameters."
- 对对手温度温和："the accuracy is unsatisfactory"（传统统计法与 multi-hyp 各一次）；"the heavy network and unsatisfactory performance restrict the usage of their method"（bianco2019quasi）——只说不够好，无贬损词。

### 标志性短语
- "To overcome aforementioned problems"（introduction.tex:52）——问题驱动的转折起手。
- "Differently, our ..."（relatedwork.tex 三段结尾，见上）。
- "by a large margin"（Abstract 末句）；"with only 16\% parameters"（Abstract、贡献第三条）——效率数字进标题级句位。
- 命名风格：概念描述型术语而非缩写——self-supervised cross-sensor training、iteration-specific attention module、lightweight head；标题也是平实陈述句 "Learning Enriched Illuminants for Cross and Single Sensor Color Constancy"。

## 3 选题品味

- 创新类型：Tier 2（极简机制替代复杂工程）为主、双轨并行——（a）把教科书物理公式变成免费标签机（数据/监督问题）；（b）把 C4 级联网络压缩（模型效率问题）。Abstract 明说双轨："we tackle these problems via two aspects"。
- 问题来源=工程痛点堆叠而非学术悖论：标注成本（需色卡/SpyderCube）、每传感器仅 0.2k–0.3k 图、CSS 导致的域差（introduction.tex:46-47）。核心洞察已经躺在 von Kries 方程里：I_raw = I_awb × ℓ 可逆，白平衡图 × 任意 ℓ = 新样本（Eq.2 + introduction.tex:52 "for each white-balanced image, a new illuminant is randomly sampled to relight the image as supervision"）。
- 实验与消融哲学：极重消融且分层展开——网络结构消融多张表（Model A–E，experiment.tex:765/796/1111）；机制可视化消融（共享 vs 非共享注意力的激活分布图 all.pdf，caption 用 "ALL of the activations from stages 2 and 3 are overlapped" 陈述证据，experiment.tex:1149）；训练组合配比扫描表（Random/No Aug./Reshuffle 各 0/50/100%，experiment.tex:598）；少样本曲线拆成 Mean / Best 25% / Worst 25% + 误差直方图（experiment.tex:388-415），主动展示长尾稳健性；训练曲线图证明稳定性（"no-relighting over-fit early and random relighting hurt the performance"，experiment.tex:721）。
- 专设辩护章节对抗审稿人替代解释：`Relationship with recurrently neural network (RNN)`（experiment.tex:728）、`Comparison with CROP`（:727）——把「这岂不是 RNN/CROP」的问题做成正式小节。
- 与其他论文关系：属其低层视觉/相机 ISP 阶段（腾讯 AI Lab 时期）；「用重光照合成监督信号」「轻量化网络」两个母题在其后续生成/编辑工作中以新形态复现（本文 conclusion 草稿里已埋 "we also believe the synthesized samples can also benefits the training process"，被注释，conclusion.tex:25 附近）。

## 4 Idea 设计

- Idea 原型：主=迁移驱动（把 von Kries 乘性关系从「解释成像」迁移为「造训练数据」），次=简化驱动（级联共享参数 + 轻量头，C4 三倍参数 → 16%）。
- 问题表述句式：非设问式，是「痛点枚举 + 双轨总起」："In this paper, we tackle these problems via two aspects."（Abstract）；引言中痛点以三连排比给出（标注贵/数据少/域差）。
- 一句话内核：既然白平衡图乘任意光源就等于一张新 RAW，那就把 sRGB 数据集反处理成白平衡图、随机采光源重光照，跨传感器 SOTA 不再需要目标传感器数据。
- 局限催生的后续（从本文自身陈述推）：随机采样的 ℓ_r 不匹配真实测试传感器的光源分布（experiment.tex:552 caption 承认 SAF "successfully fits the evaluation sensor's domain" 是靠随机光源硬拟合）——自然外推是「按目标域分布采光源」或「光源分布建模」；conclusion 草稿亦留了 "synthesized samples" 作为未来工作。
