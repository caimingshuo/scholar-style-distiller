## 2 写作修辞

**Abstract 骨架**（sections/00_abstract.tex，全文见 corpus_report §3）——system paper 式 5 步，无任何量化数字：
1. 需求定义："Accurate Story visualization requires several necessary elements, such as identity consistency across frames, the alignment..., and a reasonable layout"；
2. 痛点："Most previous works endeavor to meet these requirements by fitting a T2I model on a set of videos in the same style... However, the learned T2I models typically struggle to adapt to new characters, scenes, and styles, and often lack the flexibility to revise the layout"；
3. 方案："This paper proposes a system for generic interactive story visualization, capable of handling multiple novel characters and supporting the editing of layout and local structure"；
4. 四组件逐一展开（First(S2P)...Next(T2L)...The core component C-T2I...Finally(I2V)...）；
5. 验证："Extensive experiments and a user study are conducted to validate the effectiveness and flexibility"。

**Introduction 段落功能序列**（sections/00_intro.tex）：
- P1 任务定义+应用（:23 "Story visualization, also known as visual storytelling, is a vital method..."）；
- P2 枚举三需求 First/Second/Third（:25 "An eligible story visualization should meet several essential requirements... First, identity consistency... Second, text-visual alignment... Third, clear and logical layout."）；
- P3 前作时间线（:28-33 GAN/VAE→transformer→diffusion）；
- P4 矛盾句 + 两条局限（:36 "However, these methods encounter two unavoidable limitations."，:37/:41 分列）；
- P5 系统与四组件走读（:45-55）；
- 贡献清单仅 2 条 itemize（:57-63）：系统级一条 + 核心模块 C-T2I 一条。
- 附加指纹：intro 顶部有注释掉的写作大纲（:5-19 "% 1. describe the task... % 5. contributions"），先列段落大纲再填肉。

**Related Work 组织法**（sections/01_related_work.tex）：按任务分 2 小节（Story Visualization / Text-to-image Generation），小节内部按技术代际时间线推进（GAN/VAE 时代→transformer→diffusion，:27-34）；每小节以划界句收束——"However, these methods often struggle to generalize to novel characters... Our method targets zero-shot story visualization as well"（:36-37）；对对手温度中性偏具体（点名失败模式："Custom Diffusion aims to compose multiple concepts but falters when dealing with similar-looking concepts, such as cats and dogs" :82）。

**标志性短语**（≥2 例）：
- "X is the core component"：02_method.tex:79 "C-T2I is the core component of the story visualization system"；fig_teaser.tex:6 "The core C-T2I component takes multi-modality inputs"。
- "However, these methods encounter..." 划界：00_intro.tex:36 "However, these methods encounter two unavoidable limitations."；01_related_work.tex:36 "However, these methods often struggle to generalize to novel characters and scenes"。
- 换视角句式 "we solve X from the perspective of Y"：01_related_work.tex:83 "Since composing multiple concepts or characters in an image using inversion remains a challenge, we propose a controllable T2I model for personalized inpainting, which tackles composition from a different perspective."

**贡献清单范式**：非三条式，而是"1 个系统 + 1 个核心模块"两条（00_intro.tex:58-63）；措辞是能力声明（"can handle multiple novel characters and scenes"）而非原理发现声明。

**标题与方法命名**：冒号结构 "TaleCrafter: Interactive Story Visualization with Multiple Characters"（main.tex:49）；"-Crafter" 词缀命名；子模块用可读功能缩写 S2P/T2L/C-T2I/I2V（main.tex:47），每个缩写在公式中出现（02_method.tex:24 "S2P(S, R, F, K)"）。

## 3 选题品味

- **创新类型**：系统集成型（四个成熟机制拼装成新任务系统）+ 一处 Tier 2 级机制替换——多概念组合不从 inversion 硬解，改走"个性化 LoRA + token + inpainting"（01_related_work.tex:83；02_method.tex:94 "we use LoRA to update additional low-rank weights... instead of fine-tuning all the parameters"）。
- **问题从哪来**：痛点驱动，两个具体痛点均来自前人可指认的失败：(1) story visualization 方法绑定 FlintstonesSV 类数据集、无法泛化新角色（00_intro.tex:37 "they face challenges in generalizing to new actors and scenes, as they are trained on specific datasets"）；(2) layout/局部结构无人管、全靠文本隐式控制（00_intro.tex:41 "none of these methods take into account the third requirement"）。
- **解法哲学**：不训新基座，全部借力大模型先验——"leveraging the prior knowledge of large language and T2I models, trained on massive corpora"（abstract）；连初始化都复用现成权重（03_experiment.tex:12 "we use the encoder weights of T2I Adapter and gated self-attention weights of GLIGEN as initialization"）。
- **实验与消融哲学**：系统论文式验证，**无消融章节**（章节树：Experiments 下仅 Settings/Qualitative/Quantitative/User Study/Interactive Editing）；量化仅 2 个 CLIP 相似度指标（tab_1）+ 用户研究（tab_user_study）；关键设计是"需求→指标闭环"——开头的三需求分别映射到 text-image sim（对齐）与 image-image sim（身份一致性）两个指标（00_intro.tex:25 ↔ 03_experiment.tex:17）。定性大图承担主要说服力（5 图中 4 张是定性对比/能力展示）。
- **与他文关系线索**：C-T2I 的"每人一组 LoRA + token"身份方案与其后续 identity 定制工作（PhotoMaker 一脉）同源；本文自认多人组合不满意（02_method.tex:98-99 "The weights are not optimized for multiple characters jointly because the success rate of composing two characters is not satisfying"）——该局限是身份表征解耦方向的直接伏笔。

## 4 Idea 设计

- **Idea 原型**：主 = 迁移/组装驱动（把 LLM prompt 生成、离散扩散 layout 生成、可控 T2I 三个成熟机制迁入 story visualization 新语境，适配矛盾 = 文学描述与 T2I caption 分布的鸿沟，02_method.tex:11-13）；次 = 简化驱动（多概念组合绕开 inversion，从 inpainting 视角重述，01_related_work.tex:83）。
- **问题表述句式**：枚举"合格标准"式——"An eligible story visualization should meet several essential requirements... First... Second... Third..."（00_intro.tex:25）。先立三条合格线，再让每个组件逐条认领，这是该文的问题定义法。
- **局限催生的后续**：独立 Limitations 节自曝两点（04_conclusion.tex:2-7）——SD v1.4 小人脸差（"The quality of synthesized images heavily relies on the capability of the pre-trained model"）与 sketch 需手动提供（"Automatic sketch generation... can be treated as our future work"）；加上 LoRA 不联合训练的多人组合难题（02_method.tex:98-99），三者分别指向：更强身份定制表征（后续 PhotoMaker 类工作）、自动结构控制、以及组合式生成——均为他后续可走的路。
