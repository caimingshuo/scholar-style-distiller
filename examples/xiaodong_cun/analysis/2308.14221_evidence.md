## 2 写作修辞

- **Abstract 逐句骨架**（`sections/1_abstract.tex`，5 句闭环，无宏观开篇）：① 痛点（"Shadows often occur when we capture the documents with casual equipment…"）② 任务特殊性/矛盾（"Different from the algorithms for natural shadow removal, the algorithms in document shadow removal need to preserve the details of fonts and figures in high-resolution input"）③ 前人缺陷（"Previous works ignore this problem and remove the shadows via approximate attention and small datasets"）④ 方案双支柱（dataset + network："We handle high-resolution document shadow removal directly via a larger-scale real-world dataset and a carefully designed frequency-aware network"）⑤ 效果收束（"clearly shows a better performance than previous methods in terms of visual quality and numerical results"）。特点：直接从具体痛点进入，不写 "[field] has witnessed remarkable progress" 式宏大开场。
- **Introduction 段落功能序列**（`sections/2_introduction.tex:5-36`，4 段+清单，与教科书式五段论不同）：P1 场景与下游价值（OCR、KIE，:5）→ P2 传统物理模型方法的失败（:7）→ P3 深度方法迁移的两大障碍：分辨率矛盾 + 数据集缺失（:9-16）→ P4 解法双支柱 SD7K + FSENet（:27-29）→ 3 条贡献 bullet（:32-35）。矛盾陈述用 "On the one hand… On the other hand…" 结构（:11, :13）。
- **Related Work 组织法：粗体主题词分派 + 每段末尾划界**。三小节各以 "\noindent\textbf{…}" 开头（`sections/3_related.tex:3,8,14`），前两段按方法族点名评述（GAN-based/CNN-based），每段以 "Different from the existing datasets, we propose…"（:4）、"However, as discussed in our introduction, it needs to predict the approximate attention maps… not suitable for the high resolution inputs"（:15）收束。
- **标志性短语**：① "performs favorably against state-of-the-art methods"（`sections/2_introduction.tex:35`，贡献清单收尾句式）；② "carefully designed"（Abstract "a carefully designed frequency-aware network" + `sections/2_introduction.tex:34` "a frequency-aware network with a carefully designed network structure"）；③ "real-world"（标题与全文反复出现："large-scale real-world dataset"）。
- **贡献清单范式**（:32-35）：数据集一条 + 网络一条 + 实验对比一条的"资源-方法-验证"三件套，无概念发现类表述；第一条主语直接是 "We provide SD7K…"，第二条 "We propose FSENet…"。
- **标题与方法命名**：标题 19 词，"任务 via A 大写冠词 + B" 的资源枚举式，把卖点（大尺度真实数据集）写进标题。命名朴素可读：FSENet=Frequency-aware Shadow Erasing Net、SD7K=Shadowed Document 7K（数字入名）。
- **Limitations 显式成节**：`sections/6_experiment.tex:39-42` "the current network is not able to run on real-time or on edge devices, such as mobile phones, due to the larger parameters and heavy GPU computations. In the future, we will explore…"——诚实声明计算开销并给后续方向。

## 3 选题品味

- **创新类型：基准驱动为主（造新战场）+ 域迁移适配为辅**。核心资产是 SD7K 数据集（7k 对 2462×3699 真实文档对，"10 times larger than existing datasets"，Abstract），网络 FSENet 是围绕该域约束（高频文字细节）的适配设计，而非新机制发明。
- **问题从哪来：任务域错配的痛点/矛盾**。"natural shadow removal → document domain" 迁移失败的两个具体断点：低分辨率近似注意力撑不住高分辨率（`sections/2_introduction.tex:12` "relying on the low-resolution approximation of attention for guidance"），以及无高分辨率成对数据（:13 "there are no large-scale and high-resolution datasets designed specifically for document shadow removal at this time"）。矛盾 = 自然图像方法假设 vs 文档高保真需求。
- **实验与消融哲学：扫全分辨率区间 + 逐模块单开关**。消融表在三个数据集上做（`tabletex/6_exp/ablation.tex:22`），且定性消融一图 8 列逐个去模块（input/D3/NoTRM/NoDFE/NoSyn/NoDAT/ours/target，`figtex/6_exp/ablation.tex:52`）。对高分辨率 OOM 问题诚实处理并加脚注（`tabletex/6_exp/high.tex:198` "The asterisk (*) indicates that the result is obtained from a low-resolution result (512×512) and then improved using a super-resolution technique to address an out-of-memory error"）。还附 512 分辨率整节补充实验（`sections/supp.tex:2`）。偏工程完备型消融，无超参极端扫描或机制可视化。
- **与他论文的关系线索（最硬的一条）**：本篇直接续写他自己的 DHAN——bib 中 `cun2020towards` = "Towards ghost-free shadow removal via dual hierarchical aggregation network and shadow matting GAN, author=Cun, Xiaodong and Pun, Chi-Man"（`egbib.bib`），正文两处把自家方法接到 DHAN：`sections/2_introduction.tex:9`（自然 shadow removal 的代表引用）与 `sections/5_method.tex` 高频模块 "restore the refined details similar to \cite{cun2020towards}"。且导师 Chi-Man Pun 同列作者——2019-2022 低层视觉/阴影修复主线的正式回归。

## 4 Idea 设计

- **Idea 原型：基准驱动（主）+ 迁移驱动（次）**。一句话内核：自然图像阴影修复方法在"高分辨率文档"这一极端域上系统性失效，根因是数据缺失 + 低分辨率注意力近似，于是造最大真实配对数据集并按频率域解耦设计网络。基准驱动证据：标题、Abstract "10 times larger than existing datasets"、Related Work 专设 "Document Shadow Dataset" 小节逐一点评竞品数据集规模（`sections/3_related.tex:4`）。迁移驱动证据：`sections/2_introduction.tex:10` "several obstacles make it challenging to transfer previous natural shadow removal methods to the document domain"。
- **问题表述句式**：非设问式，而是"障碍枚举式"——"several obstacles make it challenging to…"（:10）+ "On the one hand… On the other hand…"（:11,:13）。收敛链：真实拍摄文档的阴影→高分辨率下文字/图必须保真→现有低分辨率近似失效+无数据→"直接做高分辨率：大数据集 + 频域解耦网络"。
- **局限催生的后续**：自述 Limitations（实时性/端侧不可行，`sections/6_experiment.tex:41`）指向轻量化/高效高分辨率修复；而"人工采集配对数据成本高"这一隐性局限，正可由他 2024 年的 test-time training / zero-shot 路线（2403.04258、2410.04032）来消解——后续工作恰好弃掉成对数据假设。
