## 2 写作修辞

**Abstract 逐句功能骨架**（corpus report §3 全文，9 句）：
1. 任务一句话定义："Defocus Blur Detection (DBD) aims to separate in-focus and out-of-focus regions from a single image pixel-wisely."
2. 重要性："This task has been paid much attention since bokeh effects are widely used in digital cameras and smartphone photography."
3. 矛盾（However 句）："However, identifying obscure homogeneous regions and borderline transitions in partially defocus images is still challenging."
4. 核心动作 + 首发："To solve these problems, we introduce depth information into DBD for the first time."
5. 论断式洞见："When the camera parameters are fixed, we argue that the accuracy of DBD is highly related to scene depth."
6. 类比迁移命名："Hence, we consider the depth information as the approximate soft label of DBD and propose a joint learning framework inspired by knowledge distillation."
7. 模块补充（Besides 句）："Besides, we propose a novel decoder... SRFB... SAB."
8. 量化战绩双联句："our approach outperforms 11 other state-of-the-art methods on two popular datasets. Our method also runs at over 30 fps on a single GPU, which is 2x faster than previous works."
9. 代码链接收尾："The code is available at: https://github.com/vinthony/depth-distillation"（一作本人 GitHub 句柄 vinthony）。

骨架特征：**However 句只给一个具体痛点，不用宏大叙事开场**；"for the first time" 与 "we argue that" 两个断言句是签名动作；精度+速度双指标收尾。

**Introduction 段落功能序列**（intro.tex，5 段 + bullets）：
1. 任务与下游用途（bokeh、隐私、auto-refocus）；
2. 传统手工特征路线及其失败原因（"these methods extract limited features and lack high-level semantic information. Thus, if the scene is complex, it is hard to discriminate the defocus region by particular features."，intro.tex:4）;
3. 深度学习路线回顾 + 指出共性盲点："they are still considering DBD from a 2D perspective"（intro.tex:12）;
4. 本文方案：从物理成因出发（intro.tex:14 "we start from the cause of defocus blur in the photography"），DOF 公式、depth 先验、ill-posed 的处理、SAB/SRFB 一并交代；
5. 收益句 + 贡献清单（intro.tex:19-25）。
即"承认可分解为：背景→路线A之死→路线B之盲→物理第一性→方案"的序列，**从被摄物的物理成像原理找动机**是区别于纯经验式 CV 论文的标志。

**Related Work 组织法（related.tex）：三个 `\noindent\textbf{}` 派系 + 结尾点穴。** "Traditional methods / Learning-based methods / Depth estimation and depth-assisted methods" 三段分类法（非时间线）；每段 2-4 句，褒贬克制（"have been demonstrated to be effective in some cases"→"are not robust enough in a broader variety of complex scenes"，related.tex:3）；第三段末尾给出全文 gap 句："However, previous learning-based methods only focus on learning with stronger networks~\cite{...} or dataset~\cite{...}."（related.tex:6）——以"对手只靠更大网络/数据"收束，为自己的"引入外部先验"让路。第三段还顺手引用了自己前作 `cun2018depth`（depth-assisted view synthesis，related.tex:9），是个人研究线的显式接线。

**标志性短语（≥2 例句）：**
- "simple yet effective"：`method.tex:29` "For implementation, we design a simple yet effective framework to achieve previous analysis."
- "for the first time / first attempt"：abstract "we introduce depth information into DBD for the first time"；`intro.tex:22` "To the best of our knowledge, this is the first attempt to introduce depth information in DBD..."
- 类比建模句式 "X has a similar relationship with Y"：`method.tex:27` "we find that DBD(**discrete, classification task**) and depth estimation(**continuous, regression task**) have a similar relationship with that between hard and soft labels in knowledge distillation."（正文里直接用 \textbf{} 强调 discrete/continuous 两个词）。
- 差异划界句式 "Although the structure ... are similar, the goal is totally different: ..."（`method.tex:29`）——先承认相似再断言不同，两次出现该模式（另见 intro.tex:16 "Although previous methods have discussed it..., we consider it in an efficient way"）。

**贡献清单范式（intro.tex:21-25，3 条）：** ① 首创声明（first attempt + distill as regularization）；② 两个命名模块（SAB、SRFB，各带一句功能）；③ benchmark 战绩（"two popular DBD benchmarks with 11 state-of-the-art methods"）。工程-学术混合式：首创断言在前、模块居中、数字收尾；不写"we conduct experiments and achieve good results"这类空话，每条都带专名或数字。

**标题与方法命名：** 标题 "Defocus Blur Detection via Depth Distillation"——"任务 via 机制"式 6 词标题，机制名（Depth Distillation）就是卖点，且是对经典概念 Knowledge Distillation 的一字改动（knowledge→depth），**命名即论点**。模块命名走"功能描述缩写"路线：SRFB（Selective Reception Field Block）、SAB（Supervision-guided Attention Block），可读但朴素，不玩梗。

## 3 选题品味

**创新类型：Tier 2（极简机制替代复杂工程）为主，带 Tier 1 式的"引入新信息源"断言。** 证据：竞品路线是 multi-stream / cross-layer fusion / network ensemble（BTB-F/BTB-C/CENet/DFNet），本文用单个 FCN + 一个蒸馏正则 + 一个块替换掉整个多流工程（method.tex:69 "However, these networks are still heavy and computationally inefficient. Rather than designing multi-stream networks or fusing by cross layers, we design an efficient multi-branch block for the extraction of multiple reception fields in each individual decoder."），换来 2x 速度与 SOTA（abstract 双联句）。

**问题从哪来：物理第一性 + 数据集痛点双重来源。** ① 从摄影成像原理出发：DOF≈2NCD²/f²，唯一非相机参数的是深度 D（method.tex:28）——问题不是"指标差 1%"而是"整个领域都在用 2D 视角看一个 3D 成像现象"（intro.tex:12 "still considering DBD from a 2D perspective"）；② 具体失败模式：homogeneous region 难检（abstract "identifying obscure homogeneous regions and borderline transitions... is still challenging"），实验里对应 DUT500 的弱纹理区（exp.tex:43 "500 difficult samples with obscure homogeneous, low-contrast focal regions"）。

**实验与消融哲学：**
- 公平性声明前置：不用额外数据、不做后处理（exp.tex:3 "for training, we do not use any additional samples or synthesized samples... all the results are raw outputs from the network without any post-processing"）。
- 组件逐个开关：Table 2 按 FCN→+D→+D+SRFB→+D+RFB+SA→Full 增量列（exp.tex:75），且主动做"更简单的替代品"实验——SRFB 去掉 selective attention 退化为 RFB 对照（exp.tex:121 "we also conduct the experiments to compare our SRFB with the model without selective attention~(similar to FRB)"），并诚实报告 RFB 在 CUHK100 更好、只在难的 DUT500 上胜出（exp.tex:121 "although the FRB perform better in the CUHK100 dataset, our SRFB show a much better results in DUT500"）。
- 超参扫到两端：γ ∈ {0.01, 0.05, 0.1, 1, 5} 两个数量级（exp.tex:105），展示"太大太小都变差"的谷形。
- 机制可视化验证假设：fig9 三层注意力图证明"高层管整体区域、低层管边缘细节"（exp.tex:116 caption）。
- 跨任务对照实验：把 SOD/阴影检测的 SOTA（BASNet/CPD/BDRAR/DSC）用同一框架重训来当 DBD baseline（exp.tex:52 "All the networks of relevant tasks are trained on our framework with the same input resolution and batch size"）——用"换任务模型不如换先验"支撑"深度更重要"的论点（exp.tex:62 "our network has better performance in DBD than ... BASNet or DSC because depth is more important in our task"）。
- 效率当一等公民：主表带 FPS 行（exp.tex:17），推理时间精确到 0.028s（exp.tex:3）。

**与他其他论文的关系线索：** ① Related Work 第三段引用自己前作 cun2018depth（depth-assisted view synthesis，related.tex:9），说明"深度作为辅助先验"是本人长期主线，本篇是该主线从 view synthesis 迁到 DBD；② "从强相关任务借先验/教师"的思想在 Uformer（2106.03106）中变成"从分类任务借 Transformer 机制"；③ decoder 内嵌轻量注意力块（SAB 复用 side output 做注意力）与 Uformer decoder 内嵌 modulator 是同一设计语法；④ 效率执念（FPS、2x faster）在 Uformer 里升级为 GMACs/Params 每表必列。

## 4 Idea 设计

**Idea 原型：迁移驱动（主）+ 简化驱动（副）。**
- 迁移内核一句话：把知识蒸馏的"软标签正则化"重新解释为"连续任务教离散任务"，让单目相对深度网络当 DBD 的教师——证据：method.tex:27-29 的类比论证整段（discrete/continuous 加粗对照 + Fig.2a 并排画 Knowledge Distillation vs Depth Distillation 两个同构流程图，实际看图：上图 Teacher→Distillation Loss→Student→argmax，下图 Teacher(depth)→Distillation Loss→Student，多一条 cp 虚线通向 DOF）。
- 同时是防御性迁移：直面"深度估计本身 ill-posed"的反驳（intro.tex:14 "However, the unconstrained depth estimation is an ill-posed problem. To evaluate on currently available DBD datasets and provide fair comparison..., we propose depth distillation by using a pre-trained network as regularization"）——迁移时把不可用的东西（绝对深度）换成可用的东西（相对深度、训练后丢弃教师）。
- 简化内核：SRFB 把"多流网络/跨层融合"压成一个 decoder 内分支块，用感受野数字说话：SK-Block 感受野 11 vs SRFB 43（method.tex:84 "the reception field of the Sk-Block is 11... while ours is 43"）。

**问题表述句式：断言式（we argue/we find），不用设问。** 两例：abstract "we argue that the accuracy of DBD is highly related to scene depth"；method.tex:27 "we find that DBD and depth estimation have a similar relationship with that between hard and soft labels"。收敛链：DBD 难在均质区域→均质区域判据不在 2D 外观而在 3D 位置→深度是天然先验但不可得→用预训练相对深度网络做软标签正则，测试时丢弃教师保住速度。这条链在 intro.tex:14 一段内走完。

**反直觉/标志性观察句（斜体强调）：** intro.tex:16 "\textit{a sharp patch can be regarded as blurry when we enlarge it and vice versa}"——把"模糊是相对的"这条摄影常识显式写成斜体观察，再用 SRFB 的多感受野回应它。**"观察→斜体→用模块回应"是他把常识变成贡献的手法。**

**本文局限可能催生的后续：**
- 自己承认的局限一：深度预测只在 partially defocus 图有效，all-in-focus 失效（exp.tex:129）→"检测与深度互为监督"的思路可推广到其他"某一属性可作另一属性软标签"的任务对；
- 局限二：教师固定为 Chen et al. 相对深度网络（method.tex:32），教师质量上限即先验上限→后续可换更强的自监督深度教师；
- 效率叙事（35.7 fps 但仍输 BASNet 的 90.9，exp.tex:17）→ 速度天花板明确，为后续"更轻的块"留下明确靶子（Uformer 正是把 GMACs 写进每张表）。
