## 2 写作修辞

- **Abstract 逐句骨架**（`sections/00_abstract.tex`）：(1) 系统宣言句 "We propose \textbf{FairyGen}, an automatic system for generating story-driven cartoon videos from a single child's drawing, while faithfully preserving its unique artistic style"；(2) 划界句 "Unlike previous storytelling methods that primarily focus on character consistency and basic motion, FairyGen explicitly disentangles character modeling from stylized background generation and incorporates cinematic shot design"（**"Unlike previous…, we explicitly disentangle…" 的差异声明直接进 Abstract 第 2 句**）；(3) 流程三步走：MLLM storyboard → style propagation adapter → shot design module（每步一个斜体命名模块）；(4) 动画两段：3D proxy 运动序列 + two-stage motion customization adapter（斜体命名 "timestep-shift strategy"）；(5) 收尾三连形容词 "stylistically faithful, narratively structured, and rich in smooth, natural motion, highlighting its potential for personalized and engaging story animation"。无量化数字进 Abstract——**系统型论文以「模块名清单」代替「指标清单」**。
- **Introduction 段落功能序列**（`sections/00_intro.tex`）：P1 人文叙事开场（儿童画与想象力，落到教育/艺术治疗/内容创作应用，L3）→ P2 三代方法逐一批评（L5-7：GAN/transformer 早期法 "limited by style diversity and training data constraints"；LLM 流水线 "character inconsistency, fragmented narratives, and weak motion quality"；扩散法 "struggle with cross-shot character consistency…—primarily due to reliance on real-world data priors"）→ P3 困难聚焦到单样本儿童画（L8）→ P4 本文设计哲学 "To address this, we consider cartoon story generation with a decoupled design that explicitly separates the foreground character from background synthesis"（L9）→ P5 "Building on these insights, we present FairyGen…"（L10）→ "Specifically," 三步走细节（L11-13）→ 实验收束（L14）→ 3 条贡献 bullets（L16-20）。**相比 EasyOmnimatte 少了"失败现象"段，是标准三代演进式叙事**。
- **Related Work 组织法：粗斜体小标题两派 + 每派末尾 "However/Differently" 收束**（`sections/01_related_work.tex`）："Story Generation" 派末尾 "However, all these methods focus mainly on a custom identity pipeline for characters… We argue that this kind of pipeline is still hard to customize the child-draw images… Different, to avoid the complex movement of the foreground character, we involve a 3D proxy…"；"Customized Generation" 派末尾 "However, it is still unclear how to utilize these methods in our child-draw background generation…"。对前人温度温和（"bring the universal generative priors to this problem"），批评词集中在 consistency/style/motion 三件套。**特点：把自家方法先放进 "Customized Generation" 谱系（"Our method also shares a similar inspiration with customization methods since we need to generate the customized background style and motions"）——先认亲再划界**。
- **标志性短语**（≥2 例）：
  1. "explicitly disentangles X from Y"（Abstract "explicitly disentangles character modeling from stylized background generation"；`sections/03_experiment.tex` "explicitly disentangling spatial appearance learning from temporal motion learning"）——**disentangle 是他的高频动词**。
  2. "Differently, we …"（`sections/01_related_work.tex` "Different, to avoid the complex movement…, we involve a 3D proxy"；`sections/02_method.tex:49` "Differently, we draw inspiration from traditional computer graphics pipelines"）——**用 Differently 起手划界**（甚至出现 "Different," 的误写）。
  3. "This simple yet effective strategy…"（`sections/02_method.tex` style 节末 "This simple yet effective strategy ensures that the generated scenes remain stylistically unified"）。
  4. "we hypothesize that…"（`sections/02_method.tex:75` "we hypothesize that biasing training toward noisier timesteps—later in the diffusion process—forces the model to rely on global structure rather than low-level pixel cues"）。
- **贡献清单范式：3 条 bullets = 框架 + 适配器 + 机制发现**（`sections/00_intro.tex:17-19`）：第 1 条框架宣言（"novel story video generation framework that synthesizes stylistically consistent, narratively coherent, and temporally smooth animations from a single child-drawn character image"），第 2 条模块（"style propagation adapter"），第 3 条给机制发现（"We demonstrate that shifting diffusion timesteps in the image-to-video generation significantly enhances the model's ability to learn natural, fluid motions"）——**把一个训练技巧升格为独立贡献条目**。
- **标题与方法命名**：标题 "FairyGen: Storied Cartoon Video from a Single Child-Drawn Character"——「童话前缀名 + 冒号 + 12 词应用副题」，Fairy 承接其团队" Fairy 系"命名（他早期 FairyX/买断系列，语料内 `sec/related.tex` 无直接证据，推断为组内品牌词）。子模块命名直白功能式："style propagation adapter"（PA）、"shot design module"、"two-stage motion customization adapter"、"timestep-shift strategy"。无缩写强迫症，PA 是全文唯一缩写。

## 3 选题品味

- **创新类型：Tier 2（组合+解耦的系统性创新，TOG 口味）**。不造新模型，而是把 MLLM 规划、风格传播、3D proxy、I2V 微调四种已有能力组装成自动系统；核心学术增量是两个解耦设计：(a) 前景/背景解耦且**风格从前景传向背景**（"Unlike traditional stylization approaches that transfer style from a reference background, our method propagates style from the character to the background, requiring the background to inherit the foreground's visual attributes"，`sections/02_method.tex` style 节）；(b) 身份/运动解耦的两阶段 LoRA 训练（`sections/02_method.tex:57-59`）。
- **问题来源：从"任务反差"来的痛点**——儿童画是极端 OOD 输入（"single-example scenarios where the input style diverges significantly from training data"，`sections/00_intro.tex:8`），现有 identity-first 流水线对此失效；同时复杂人体运动是 I2V 模型的能力边界，于是借 3D 中间表示绕开生成先验的短板（"restricted by the generative prior of the image-to-video diffusion model, generating id-consistent and coherent motion is inherently challenging"，`sections/02_method.tex:50`）。**生成模型不行的地方用传统 CG 补**——先验的边界意识非常清醒。
- **实验与消融哲学：为新任务自建评测面 + 三消融对齐三贡献**。因 "there are no previous baselines for this task"（`sections/03_experiment.tex:20`），自组对比集（MEVG/Vlogger/DreamVideo + Animate-X/深度引导法），CLIP 风格距离 + VBench 两指标 + 24 人 user study（3360 opinions）；消融恰好三条：Style Adapter（DoRA vs LoRA vs PA）、Motion Adapter（直接训 vs 两阶段）、Time-Shift（uniform vs 不同 μ）——**贡献条目与消融一一对应**。诚实的失败披露："our visual impression score (0.3251) is slightly lower than B-LoRA (0.3429), we attribute this to B-LoRA's photorealistic output, which may be perceived as more visually appealing than child-style cartoon imagery"（`sections/03_experiment.tex`）；专属 Limitation 图（`figures/texs/limitation.tex:5` "the proposed method might only generate a still background with the animated foreground motion"）。工程轻量：1×L20、风格 120 分钟、运动 180 分钟（`sections/03_experiment.tex:8`）。弱项：无逐组件数字消融表，消融以定性图为主。
- **与其他论文的关系**：(a) 与 EasyOmnimatte 同款配方——冻结预训练大模型 + LoRA 定制 + 分阶段/分专家解耦（本篇 identity/motion 两阶段 ↔ EasyOmnimatte effect/quality 双专家），且都基于 Wan/MMDiT 系基座；(b) 引用自家 VideoCrafter1/2 作为 customization 谱系起点（`sections/01_related_work.tex` "fine-tuning the text-to-image/video models~\cite{videocrafter1, videocrafter2, wanvideo…} for customizing usage is natural and practical"）——**从「克服数据限制训模型」演化到「不训模型、只做适配」**；(c) 3D proxy 沿用 DrawingSpinUp（儿童画转 3D），延续其对"单张图→可动角色"这条线的兴趣；(d) 局限段把后续路指明：多角色多 proxy、更强 rigging（unirig）、换 HunyuanVideo、加相机运动控制（`sections/03_experiment.tex` Limitation and Future Work）。

## 4 Idea 设计

- **Idea 原型：简化/解耦驱动（主）+ 迁移驱动（辅）**。主原型是把「身份-运动-风格-镜头」四股缠绕的生成问题拆成两条可分别定制的流水线；辅原型是**方向反转式迁移**：风格定制历来"参考背景→风格化"，他反转成"前景角色→背景风格传播"（`sections/02_method.tex` style 节）——与 EasyOmnimatte 的「擦除⇒可保留」反演同构，都是把既定信息流向倒过来。另有现象驱动的小部件：timestep-shift 来源于对扩散训练均匀采样 t 的假设质疑（"we hypothesize that biasing training toward noisier timesteps… forces the model to rely on global structure"，`sections/02_method.tex:75`），并配 μ 扫描消融（"μ = 6 provides better results than uniform sampling and other hyperparameters"，`sections/03_experiment.tex`）。
- **问题表述句式**："how to generate a fully stylized, long cartoon video from a single child-drawn character while faithfully maintaining character consistency…?"（`sections/02_method.tex:11` 的 goal 句）；划界句式 "Unlike previous storytelling methods that primarily focus on X, we explicitly disentangle…"。**「从最小输入（单张儿童画）生成最大输出（多镜头故事片）」的极小输入-极大输出设问**。
- **收敛链**：story visualization 一致性老问题 → 发现儿童画是所有方法的共同盲区 → 判定 identity-first 流水线在风格-运动双定制下必坏 → 拆成前景（3D proxy 保证运动）/背景（风格传播）两支 → 各自配一个轻量适配器。收敛的剪刀是「解耦」：每次遇到两因素纠缠（身份/运动、前景/背景、效果/主体），他都切成两阶段或双专家——这是跨篇稳定的思维动作。
- **本文局限催生的后续**：(1) 静止背景问题（limitation 图）→ 背景也纳入运动定制/相机控制（他自己点了 RecamMaster、HunyuanVideo）；(2) 3D proxy 重建失败的角色（"The foreground character may not always be correctly reconstructed by the 3D proxy"）→ 更强 rigging 或运动先验直接学习；(3) 单角色 → 多角色多 proxy 的调度与交互；(4) timestep-shift 假设只扫了 μ → 噪声调度与运动学习的系统研究（可成为独立分析型论文）。
- **可复用的设计动作**：拿到一个定制化需求时，问"风格/运动/身份哪个是真正的新信息？"——把不是新信息的部分冻结掉（identity LoRA 冻结后再学 motion 残差，`sections/02_method.tex:69-72`），只用最小参数承载新信息。
