## 2 写作修辞

**Abstract 逐句骨架**（main.tex:108-130 定稿文本，见 corpus_report 第 3 节）——**方法宣言先行式，砍掉背景铺垫**：
1. "In this paper, we present Uformer, an effective and efficient Transformer-based architecture for image restoration, in which we build a hierarchical encoder-decoder network using the Transformer block."（第一句即宣言；原背景句 "Transformer has shown remarkable success…" 被注释掉，main.tex 注释行 890）
2. "In Uformer, there are two core designs. First, we introduce a novel locally-enhanced window (LeWin) Transformer block, which performs non-overlapping window-based self-attention instead of global self-attention."
3. "Second, we propose a learnable multi-scale restoration modulator in the form of a multi-scale spatial bias to adjust features in multiple layers of the Uformer decoder."
4. "Powered by these two designs, Uformer enjoys a high capability for capturing both local and global dependencies for image restoration."（承上启下总结句）
5. "To evaluate our approach, extensive experiments are conducted on several image restoration tasks, including image denoising, motion deblurring, defocus deblurring and deraining."
6. "Without bells and whistles, our Uformer achieves superior or comparable performance compared with the state-of-the-art algorithms." + "The code and models are available at~\url{…}"（SOTA 声明 + 开源链接收尾）

**Introduction 段落功能序列**（intro.tex 定稿，非注释部分）——比五段论更紧凑的 4 段+清单：
- P1（line 20-22）：应用背景一句带过 → 一句点破瓶颈："Recent state-of-the-art methods~\cite{…} are mostly ConvNets-based, which achieve impressive results but show a limitation in capturing long-range dependencies."
- P2（line 24）：直切本文："In this paper, we aim to leverage the capability of self-attention in feature maps at multi-scale resolutions to recover more image details. To this end, we present Uformer…"，并交代继承关系："built upon an elegant architecture UNet, where we modify the convolution layers to Transformer blocks while keeping the same overall hierarchical encoder-decoder structure and the skip-connections."
- P3（line 46-47）："We propose two core designs to make Uformer suitable for image restoration tasks. First, we propose the **L**ocally-**e**nhanced **Win**dow (LeWin) Transformer block…"（含诚实承认："We also notice that recent works~\cite{leff,li2021localvit} use the similar design for different tasks."）
- P4（line 51-54）："Second, we propose a learnable multi-scale restoration modulator to handle various image degradations."
- P5（line 62-67）："Based on the above two designs, without bells and whistles, \eg, the multi-stage or multi-scale framework and the advanced loss function, our simple U-shaped Transformer structure achieves state-of-the-art performance…"，随后逐任务报具体 dB（0.14/0.09/1.04/3.74），收尾展望句 "We expect our work will encourage further research to explore Transformer-based architectures for image restoration."
- Contributions（line 69-76）：**朴素 3 条 bullet**——框架（"We present Uformer, a general and superior U-shaped Transformer…"）、模块（"an extra light-weight learnable multi-scale restoration modulator"）、实验（"Extensive experiments show that Uformer establishes new state-of-the-arts…"）。是"提出 X"式而非认知发现式。

**Related Work 组织法**（related.tex）：两大 `\textbf{}` 主题分类段（"Image Restoration Architectures" / "Vision Transformers"，related.tex:3,10），按技术路线而非时间线；每段以"我们的差异"收束（related.tex:8 "Our Uformer also applies…"; related.tex:39 "In contrast, we design a general U-shaped Transformer-based structure…"）。对前人温度中性偏褒："The pioneering work of ViT"（line 15），点穴但不刻薄："However, IPT relies on pretraining on a large-scale synthesized dataset and multi-task learning for good performance."（line 36）

**标志性短语（≥2 例）**：
- "**without bells and whistles**"：abstract（main.tex 定稿末段）+ intro.tex:62，双次出现，用于声明"无 trick 的 SOTA"。
- "**We expect our/that our work will encourage further research to explore…**"：intro.tex:67；同款句在 abstract 注释行 914 "We expect that our work will encourage future research to explore Transformer-based architectures for low-level vision tasks."；conclusion.tex:18 变体 "We look forward to investigating Uformer for more applications."
- "**effective and efficient**"：abstract 第一句 + intro.tex:24 "an effective and efficient Transformer-based structure"。
- "**Powered by these two designs**"（abstract）/"Based on the above two designs"（intro.tex:62）：First/Second 双设计 + 收束句模板。

**标题与方法命名**：`\title{Uformer: A General U-Shaped Transformer for Image Restoration}`（main.tex:84）——冒号结构「方法名: A General X for Y」；main.tex:83-86 保留 3 个废弃标题（"Uformer: A General U-shape Transformer-based Architecture…"、"UT: Transformer for Image Restoration"、"An Empirical Study of Transformer-based Network Structure for Image Restoration"），显示命名从「empirical study」收敛到「general 架构宣言」。方法名 Uformer（U-shaped+Transformer，可读、朴素）；变体借 ViT 惯例 T/S/B（exp.tex:45 "Uformer-T (Tiny), Uformer-S (Small), and Uformer-B (Base)"）。

## 3 选题品味

- **创新类型：Tier 2 迁移驱动为主**——把高层视觉已验证的 window attention（Swin）+ UNet 层级结构迁到 low-level restoration，真正的贡献是解决适配矛盾。矛盾表述集中一句（method.tex:69）："There are two main challenges to apply Transformer for image restoration. First, the standard Transformer architecture computes self-attention globally…It is unsuitable to apply global self-attention on high-resolution feature maps. Second, the local context information is essential for image restoration tasks since the neighborhood of a degraded pixel can be leveraged to restore its clean version, but previous works suggest that Transformer shows a limitation in capturing local dependencies."
- **问题从哪来：算力公平性焦虑**。对标 IPT 需大规模预训练+多任务学习（related.tex:36），注释稿更直白："IPT: 32 V100 / Ours: Single GPU"（intro.tex:94-98 被注释的对比表）与 "under the same settings (training datasets and computational resources) as the CNN-based works"（related.tex:34 注释稿）。即：不是发明新算子，而是在公平预算下证明"Transformer 首次在 restoration 可行"。
- **实验与消融哲学**：(a) 与**自造同预算对照物**打，而非只打 SOTA——把 LeWin 全换 ResBlock 构造 UNet-T/S/B 镜像对照（exp.tex:586-599），再构造单尺度 ViT 对照证明层级必要性（exp.tex:651）；(b) **位置扫描**式消融："Where to enhance locality?" 比较 locality 加在 W-MSA vs FFN，FFN +0.03/+0.07 dB 而加进 attention 反而 -0.02 dB（exp.tex:673-676, tab:locality）；(c) 组件开关跨 3 任务验证泛化：modulator 在 GoPro +0.46 / SIDD +0.03 / SPAD +0.41 dB（exp.tex:703, tab:pe）；(d) 报告弱结果不隐瞒："We also try the shifted-window strategy~\cite{swin}…which gives only slightly better results."（method.tex:117）。
- **对他论文的关系线索**：modulator 灵感显式回指 StyleGAN 噪声注入（method.tex:194 "This is consistent with the previous work StyleGAN~\cite{stylegan} using a multi-scale noise term adding to the convolution features"）——暴露其 GAN/生成式背景；LeFF 承认沿用 leff/LocalViT（intro.tex:47）；limitation 点名 image-to-image translation、super-resolution 未评估（conclusion.tex:18），指向其后续低层视觉战场。

## 4 Idea 设计

- **Idea 原型：迁移驱动（主）+ 简化驱动（次）**。迁移内核一句话：restoration 是高分辨率密集任务，global attention 不可行 → 用 window attention + UNet 层级让"低分辨率上的 window attention 感受野足够大"，即"Since we design Uformer as a hierarchical architecture, our window-based self-attention at low resolution feature maps works on larger receptive fields and is sufficient to learn long-range dependencies."（method.tex:117）。简化内核：单阶段、单尺度输入、Charbonnier 单损失（method.tex:52-56）、无预训练无多任务、显式声明 "without bells and whistles, \eg, the multi-stage or multi-scale framework and the advanced loss function"（intro.tex:62）。
- **问题表述句式**：目标宣言式而非设问式——"In this paper, we aim to [leverage X in Y] to [recover more image details]. To this end, we present [Name], an effective and efficient [架构类] for [任务]."（intro.tex:24）。矛盾用 "There are two main challenges… First… Second…" 双挑战句式（method.tex:69）。
- **本文局限可能催生的后续**：① 自述未评估 SR/image-to-image translation（conclusion.tex:18）→ 窗口注意力通用 restoration backbone（如 SwinIR 一类）与 I2I 任务迁移；② window shift 收益甚微（method.tex:117）→ 窗口交互机制可再设计；③ modulator 在退化越"空间结构化"的任务上增益越大（去噪仅 +0.03 dB vs 去模糊 +0.46 dB，exp.tex:703）→ 退化自适应调制的后续空间；④ limitation 的图像取证伦理段落（conclusion.tex:18 "The techniques may destroy the original patterns for camera identification and multi-media copyright, which hurts the authenticity for image forensics"）与其本人后续关注 restoration/生成对真实性的影响一致。
- **隐性筛选标准可见项**：敢砍——skip-connection 三个变体实验后结论是"都差不多，用最朴素的 Concat"（exp.tex:707 注释稿 "These three skip-connections achieve similar results, and concatenating features gets slightly better performance. We adopt the feature concatenation as the default setting."），对应正文 method.tex 中该节整体被 `\iffalse` 删除——宁可正文更简洁也不留无增量章节。
