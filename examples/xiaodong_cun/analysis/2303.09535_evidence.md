## 2 写作修辞

### Abstract 逐句骨架（corpus report §3，含修订批注）
1. 背景："The diffusion-based generative models have achieved remarkable success in text-based image generation."
2. 矛盾："However, since it contains enormous randomness in generation progress, it is still challenging to apply such models for real-world visual content editing, especially in videos."
3. 提案+双零卖点："we propose \texttt{FateZero}, a zero-shot text-based editing method on real-world videos without per-prompt training or use-specific mask."
4. 核心发现句（现象驱动）："we find the intermediate attention maps during inversions store better structure and motion information. We thus reform them to temporally casual attention and replace them in the generation progress."
5. 技术清单（"To edit videos consistently, we propose several techniques... Firstly... To further minimize... Furthermore..."三步排比）。
6. First 宣言收束："Yet succinct, our method is the first one to show the ability of zero-shot text-driven video style and local attribute editing from the trained text-to-image model." 末句 "Extensive experiments demonstrate our superior temporal consistency and editing capability than previous works."

### Introduction 段落功能序列（01_intro.tex）
- P1（:5）扩散模型成功 → 编辑机会（一段 2 句，无铺垫）。
- P2（:7）既有图像编辑方法的两段式管线（DDIM 反演→按新提示去噪），点名 pix2pix-zero/pnp/p2p 三条路线。
- P3（:17-21）三挑战枚举，用 First/Second/Finally 排比："First, there are no publicly available generic text-to-video models"；"Second... these methods still need atlas learning, keyframe selection, and per-prompt tunning... the shape editing is still a big challenge"；"Finally... the inverted noise in the $T$ step might break the motion and structure of the original video because of error accumulation"——第三条即本文切入点。
- P4（:40）提案："we propose \texttt{FateZero}, a simple yet effective method for zero-shot video editing since we do not need to train for each target prompt individually and have no user-specific mask."
- P5（:43）两设计走读（存储反演期注意力 + 时空注意力改造），关键句 "Specifically, we find these self-attention blocks store better motion information and the cross-attention can be used as a threshold mask for self-attention blending spatially."
- P6（:49）"Powered by our novel designs, we can directly edit the style and the attribute of the real-world video..."，指认 teaser 两行结果。
- 贡献 4 条（:55-64）：首条 "first framework" 宣言 + 机制条 + 组件条 + 应用条，末条 "We show extensive applications of our method in video style editing, video local editing, video object replacement, \etc."

### Related Work 组织法（02_related.tex）
- `\noindent\textbf{Video Editing.}` / `\textbf{Image and Video Generation Models.}` / `\textbf{Image Editing in Diffusion Model.}` 三段式粗体段首（:7、:23、:33）——按任务派别而非时间线。
- 每段以对手的明确缺陷收束："However, the 2d atlas lacks 3d motion perception to support shape editing, and prompt-specific optimization is required."（:16）；"a naive frame-wise application of these image methods to video results in flickering and inconsistency among frames."（:39）。

## 3 选题品味

- 创新类型：Tier 1/2 交界——不训练新模型，而是重新解读既有管线中被丢弃的中间信号（反演期注意力），属于「认知重解读 + 极简机制替换复杂工程」（替换 per-prompt 优化、用户 mask、atlas 学习三条昂贵路线）。
- 问题来源=新模型红利 + 旧管线矛盾：一是扩散模型兴起但「no publicly available generic text-to-video models」（01_intro.tex:17），所以选择复用开源图像模型；二是 DDIM 反演→生成的两段式独立管线存在误差累积、"inverted noise in the T step might break the motion and structure"（:21）。
- 实验与消融哲学：
  - 无现成对手就自组基线："Since there are no available zero-shot video editing methods based on diffusion models, we build the following four state-of-the-art baselines for comparison."（04_exp.tex:53）——用组件拼装 4 个组合基线并保证公平（"we use the same timesteps fusion parameters as ours"，:59）。
  - 量化指标=CLIP 派生（Tem-Con、Frame-Acc）+ 3 项 20 人用户研究（04_exp.tex:61-62），指标命名加粗定义（\textbf{`Tem-Con'}）。
  - 消融靠注意力图可视化说话：反演期 vs 重建期注意力的 cross-attention 热图对比（"The attention maps obtained from the reconstruction stage fail to detect the boat's position"，figs/ablation_inversion_attn.tex:6）；全融合 vs 掩码融合的定性消融（ablation_masked_attention.tex:16），先在玩具图像上证明再 "here, we ablate these designs in the video"（04_exp.tex:81）。
  - 对基线的批评落点在逐帧抖动与优化成本："Frame-wise Null inversion achieves local editing at the cost of 500-iterations optimization for each frame but is still temporally inconsistent."（04_exp.tex:70）
- 与其他论文关系：腾讯 AI Lab 实习生一作 + 存晓东为共同贡献者的「导师带做」模式；零训练、复用预训练模型、把中间表征当控制信号的思路，与其低层视觉时期「免费监督信号」（2203.11068 的重光照造标签）一脉相承；局限性段自认受制于 one-shot 视频扩散模型，为「等更强视频扩散模型」留下续作接口（10_conclusion.tex："We will test our method on the generic pretrained video diffusion model for better editing abilities."）。

## 4 Idea 设计

- Idea 原型：主=现象驱动（反演期注意力存有更好结构/运动信息——由注意力热图可视化发现，"We observe this huge difference in attention maps between inversion and reconstruction exists in most videos." 04_exp.tex:85），次=矛盾驱动（两段式反演→生成管线丢弃结构信息且误差累积）。
- 问题表述句式：非设问式；以「问题仍开放」陈述句收敛——"However, how to edit real-world content using this model is still unclear."（02_related.tex:30）；Abstract 用 "we find ..." 现象句替代设问。
- 一句话内核：把 DDIM 反演各步存下的自注意力/交叉注意力直接替换进生成期，并用源提示词的交叉注意力阈值掩码混合自注意力——零训练、零用户 mask 的视频编辑。
- 局限催生的后续（本文自述）："it is difficult to generate totally new motion (e.g., `swim'→`fly') or very different shape (e.g., `swan'→`pterosaur')"（10_conclusion.tex）→ 等更强视频扩散先验后重做；shape 编辑受制于 one-shot 模型 → 通用视频模型版本；附录 limitation 图（12_appendix.tex:71 limitation-cropped.pdf）已被规划。
